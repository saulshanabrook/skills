## Classification Notes

### Convergent

Needed and already well covered. Preserve, tighten, and avoid disproportionate rewrite effort.

### Divergent

Needed but under-specified, inconsistent, ambiguous, or contradicted. Produce an RFC/spec or ADR before implementation.

### Absent

Expected by readers but missing or barely present. Add to open questions or create a scoped proposal.

### Deferred

Intentionally outside current scope. Record the re-entry trigger.

### Blocked

Cannot be specified without more primary evidence or a decision. Name the missing evidence or decision owner.
