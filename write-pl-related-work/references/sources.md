# Find the source for a related-work decision

Use this index to choose a relevant original, open its exact section, and read enough context to settle the decision. The short per-source records distinguish explicit advice, observed organization, and our adaptations; they are navigation aids rather than substitutes for full text. The five guides and sixteen paper examples were reread in November 2026. This is a bounded example corpus, not a representative study of PL norms.

## Check the claim boundary

Apply the checks relevant to the disputed sentence, not every check to every edit:

- **Mechanism and relationship:** separate an implemented feature, prototype, proposed direction, semantic connection, and your own analogy. Relational E-matching's [Discussion](primary/papers/relational-ematching.pdf#page=19) deliberately includes several of these roles.
- **Guarantee and artifact:** distinguish type preservation, semantic preservation, proof production, certificate checking, and mechanization. State the component, assumptions, and extent of the result rather than promoting a local theorem to whole-stack correctness. HoTTSQL's [§6.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1607.04822v2#page=12) and the [Aeneas record](primary/papers/aeneas-2022-long-version.md) illustrate scope distinctions.
- **Transformation and packaging:** an extension distributed as a plugin or wrapper may still change an evaluator, compiler, or runtime. Establish the actual transformation boundary from its implementation description; do not infer it from the package label.
- **Performance:** check timed operations, baseline, workload/cohort, and aggregation. Capture, extraction, and checking may be separate measurements; a runtime ratio is not an added percentage. Read the evaluation definitions, captions, and qualifications before claiming similar overhead across systems.

These operational checks are our adaptation. The example sections illustrate careful distinctions; they do not themselves prescribe a universal evidence checklist. Historical priority, capability, deployment, and maintenance judgments must be verified before reuse as current facts.

## Keep the evidence and guidance distinct

The writing guides give advice and disagree on placement. The papers show how particular authors organized comparisons. This skill adapts both for source-grounded manuscript collaboration. None establishes fixed source counts, section counts, paragraph lengths, a compulsory opening/ending formula, or a requirement to describe every predecessor as deficient. A narrow task can use one relevant passage; a disputed mechanism may need deeper reading. Do not reread all twenty-one sources after every tweak.
