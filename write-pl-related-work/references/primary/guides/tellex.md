# Stefanie Tellex — writing advice

**Source/version:** [Writing a Technical Paper](https://h2r.cs.brown.edu/writing-a-technical-paper/#post-688), October 24, 2019. Checked November 10, 2026.

**Read:** the second article subheading, [Related Work](https://h2r.cs.brown.edu/writing-a-technical-paper/#post-688:~:text=Related%20Work), and the following [Technical Approach](https://h2r.cs.brown.edu/writing-a-technical-paper/#post-688:~:text=Technical%20Approach). The page supplies a stable article ID but no heading IDs; these text fragments are optional browser navigation, not invented HTML anchors.

**Explicit advice:** Prepare individual summaries and comparisons, then group them and synthesize. Condense when space requires; a particularly close predecessor may warrant a paragraph. Tellex prefers early placement but accepts later technical comparison. Motivate technical components through the problems they solve.

**Adaptation/limits:** Separate detailed source notes from final presentation. Neither a paper-catalog requirement nor a ban on individual-paper paragraphs follows. Her robotics-specific introduction pattern and citation-package preference should not override a PL venue's needs and tooling.
