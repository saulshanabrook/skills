# Jennifer Widom — writing advice

**Source/version:** [Tips for Writing Technical Papers](https://cs.stanford.edu/people/widom/paper-writing.html), January 2006; page describes limited 2009 revisions and its 2012 presentation. Checked November 10, 2026.

**Read:** [placement](https://cs.stanford.edu/people/widom/paper-writing.html#related), [introduction](https://cs.stanford.edu/people/widom/paper-writing.html#intro), [body](https://cs.stanford.edu/people/widom/paper-writing.html#body), and [citations](https://cs.stanford.edu/people/widom/paper-writing.html#citations).

**Explicit advice:** Put related work early when a concise comparison suffices or immediate positioning is important; put it later when comparison depends on technical content. An early summary can precede detailed treatment. Present the argument and results rather than the discovery history, distinguish inherited background, and check bibliography consistency.

**Adaptation/limits:** Choose placement from reader prerequisites and the purpose of comparison. Treat the introduction template as an available scaffold, not a mandatory paragraph count. Widom does not prescribe a fixed related-work hierarchy or a paper-versus-theme template.
