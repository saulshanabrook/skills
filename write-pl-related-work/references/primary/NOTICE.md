# Primary-source notices and attribution

This directory contains twenty-one reading/rights records, nine unchanged paper PDFs, and the unchanged Diátaxis how-to source and license. The records link original locations and exact versions, distinguish source advice or observed organization from our adaptations, and document the relevant redistribution evidence. Pointer-only records remain useful without the optional personal reading cache.

These third-party originals retain their own terms. The skill repository's MIT license does not relicense them. Do not remove original notices or alter source files to make them agree with a skill update.

See the per-source records for author names, original-source URLs, version, section/page links, license links, and evidence. CC BY originals require applicable attribution and notices; adaptations of the Diátaxis source require compatible ShareAlike terms. The copied source commit is documented without asserting which commit the website deployed.

The pointer-only sources have no unrestricted public-copy grant verified for this package. Their full text is available through official links and, on the owner's installation, optional local-only reading copies outside this directory. That cache is ignored by Git and is not a public publication payload.

[checksums.sha256](checksums.sha256) records the bytes of the eleven bundled original files. From this directory, `shasum -a 256 -c checksums.sha256` checks that they remain unchanged. Reading notes and this notice are separate from the originals.
The two copied Diátaxis RST originals preserve upstream formatting, including trailing whitespace; use the checksums to verify their verbatim bytes.
