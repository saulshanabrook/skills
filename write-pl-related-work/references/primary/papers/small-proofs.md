# Small Proofs from Congruence Closure

Oliver Flatt, Samuel Coward, Max Willsey, Zachary Tatlock, Pavel Panchekha. FMCAD 2022; inspected arXiv:2209.03398v1, September 7, 2022.

**Read:** [§II Background and Related Work, PDF pp.2–3](small-proofs.pdf#page=2); no printed page labels. [Exact original PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2209.03398v1). Read the two-column flow through the top of p.3; Figure 1 is physically above the section heading.

**Observed organization:** Definitions and a running example precede explanation-tree machinery and the nearest proof-shrinking method. The closing comparison isolates added cost and dependence on the initial proof's retained alternatives.

**Adaptation and limit:** Teach enough shared machinery for the technical difference to be intelligible. Merged background is a counterexample to mandatory immediate contribution-first prose. Proof minimization here does not establish that other proof-producing architectures yield smaller proofs.
