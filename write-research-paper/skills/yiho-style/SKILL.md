# Yiho style: prose revision

This local summary paraphrases [Yiho Zhang's style skill](https://github.com/yihozhang/style/blob/main/SKILL.md), which draws on Joseph M. Williams and Joseph Bizup's *Style: Lessons in Clarity and Grace*.

## Apply a focused revision pass

- Inspect roughly the first seven or eight words of each sentence: can readers quickly find the subject and verb? Do successive subjects follow a coherent set of topics?
- Express actions through verbs and name the relevant actors. Unpack dense noun sequences; retain passive voice when it improves the reader's orientation.
- Connect familiar information to new information. Give important new material a strong sentence ending.
- Remove repetition, filler, and unnecessary framing. Preserve qualifications that carry meaning.
- Keep the main clause easy to follow, then add necessary complexity. Use parallel forms and varied sentence lengths where they help.
- Make the reader's problem and the document's point apparent; organize sections around a visible rationale.
- Preserve grammatical accuracy without enforcing stylistic folklore. Keep responsibility and consequential stakes clear.

These are diagnostics for revision, not requirements that every sentence have the same shape. Preserve technical meaning and the author's voice.
